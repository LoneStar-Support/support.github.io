Volunteer coaches: your steadiness can change a family’s whole season.

If you have lived long enough to learn humility—and you’re willing to be trained—we’d love to meet you. LoneStar Support volunteers are not “fixers.” You’re skilled listeners and encouragers who help couples and co-parents find their way back to teamwork.

What we’re looking for

  • ✦Experience navigating complex relationships (personally or professionally).
  • ✦A commitment to confidentiality, cultural humility, and trauma-informed care.
  • ✦Availability for training, supervision, and approximately 3–5 hours monthly.

Training you can trust

Volunteers complete onboarding in coaching ethics, family systems basics, accessibility mindsets, and escalation protocols when families need clinical or crisis resources beyond our scope.
