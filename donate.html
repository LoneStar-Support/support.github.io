Donation FAQ

Will I receive a tax receipt?

Yes—online gifts typically generate an automatic email receipt. For offline gifts, we’ll mail or email acknowledgment with our legal name and EIN.

Can I donate in honor of someone?

Absolutely. Add a tribute note in your provider’s form fields (or email us) and we can send a thoughtful acknowledgment if you wish.

Do you accept employer matching?

We’re working to register with common matching platforms—ask your employer and contact us if you need documentation.
